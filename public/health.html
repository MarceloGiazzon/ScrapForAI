One card per target profile: the last state of each library routine, its trend and flakiness, performance alerts, the regression vault and the schedules. A target is red when something that counts failed in its last run, yellow when nothing failed but something needs a person, green otherwise. Quarantined routines and items are shown apart and never turn a target red. This screen starts nothing. A runner or an admin can change a routine's status (quarantine, release, retire) with the Status… button: it saves the same steps as a new version, and the reason goes to the audit log.
